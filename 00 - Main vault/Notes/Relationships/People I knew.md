## General note:
The people I know are devided into few classes that may or may not interact with each other as follow:

[[My Family]] is composed of the following classes:
- AtomicFamily : Father, Mother, bros & siss
- NuclearFamily: Wife & kids
- DadF: father’s fam
- MomF: mother’s fam
- InLawsW: wife’s fam
- InLawB: bro’s inlaws
- InLawS: sis’s inlaws
- BigFam: far but still relative

[[My Friends]], are classed into these classes:
- super close friends, 
- close friends, 
- casual contacts 
- acquaintances 

[[My Professional circle]] is devided into:
- classmates, 
- collaborators
- colleagues, 
- mentees.
- subordinates
- supervisors, 
- teachers
- teammates

[[My Community]] is devided into;
- neighbors 
- religious
- reputation
- cultural groups.

Mark each contact as:
- **Active** (currently in touch)
- **Dormant** (no contact but can reconnect)
- **Past** (no realistic chance of reconnection)
- Known (no real established connection)

In order to make it easier for me to track who’s who, for each contact in each of these classes it should have a tag of it
