# Atomic habits

An Easy & Proven Way to Build Good Habits & Break Bad Ones

---
#### 1st day lecture
- "A Habit is a routine or a behavior that is performed regularly -and in many cases, automatically".
- 1% Better every day
  - 1% worse every day for one year: 0.99^365 = 0.02551796445 (x times) worst
  - 1% better every day for one year: 1.01^365 = 37.7834343329 (x times) better
- Luck matters
- Habits are not the only thing that influence your success, but they are probably the most important factor that is withig your controll.
- Self-improvement strategy => focus on what you can control
- We make a few changes, but the results never seem to come quickly and so we slide back into our previous routines.
- You get what you repeat
- Your habits can compund for you or against you.
- Learning one idea won't make you a genius, but a commitment to lifelong learning can be transformative.
- THe more you help others, the more people want to help you.
- People reflect your behavior back to you.
- The more you think of yourself as worthless, stupid, or ugly, the more you condition yourself to interpret life that way.
- When you finally break through the "Plateau of Latent Potential", people will call it an overnight success.
- The outside world only sees the most dramatic event rather than all that preceded it.
- Goals are about the results you want to achieve.
- Systems are about the processes that lead to those results.
- Goals are good for settings a direction, but systems are better for making progress.
- It was only when the British cyclist implemented a system of continuous small improvements that they achieved a different outcome.
- You treat a symptom without addressing the cause.
